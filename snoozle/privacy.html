Snoozle – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026 (adds the AI features, voice alarm set-up, bedtime stories and Premium subscriptions)

Snoozle ("the app") is an alarm clock and sleep app for iPhone and Android, made by RVV Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, who processes it, and the choices you have. Contact: under.rated.ruan@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No account, and we never ask for your name, email, contacts or photos.
  • Your alarms, sleep estimates, stats, streaks and settings are stored on your device.
  • AI features (morning note, weekly sleep summary, describing your alarms) send only a few numbers or the sentence you say or type to our own server, which asks Anthropic's Claude AI for a reply. They are optional and can be switched off in Settings ▸ AI features.
  • When you set alarms by voice, your speech is turned into text on your device where possible; only the text is sent.
  • The free version shows ads and measures app usage (details below). Premium removes the ads.
  • Payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • Snoozle's sleep information is an estimate from your phone's movement for general wellness. It is not medical advice.

AI features in detail

  • Optional and in your control. The first time a feature would send data, Snoozle explains what is sent and asks. You can switch AI features off at any time in Settings; nothing is sent while it is off, and Snoozle uses its own built-in text instead.
  • What is never sent: your name, alarm labels, contacts, calendar, photos, location, advertising ID, or any audio.
  • How it works: requests go over HTTPS to our server (hosted by Cloudflare), which forwards only the data listed above to Anthropic's Claude AI and returns the reply. The AI key is kept on the server and never in the app.
  • Retention: our server does not store the content of your requests; it keeps only a daily count per random install ID to apply usage limits, which expires automatically. Anthropic processes requests to produce the reply under its commercial terms, does not use them to train its models, and keeps them only for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring (Anthropic privacy policy).
  • Wellness, not medicine. AI-written notes and summaries describe your sleep estimates in plain words. They are not medical advice, and Snoozle filters out medical wording.
  • AI-written text can occasionally be inaccurate. Alarms suggested from your words are always shown to you to confirm or change before they are created.

Bedtime stories and wake-up questions

Bedtime stories are written for Snoozle (with the help of AI) and narrated with a synthetic voice. The story audio is downloaded from our website (ruanvv24.github.io, hosted by GitHub) the first time you play it, then plays offline; downloading it sends only a normal web request (such as your IP address), which GitHub may log. Wake-up trivia questions are built into the app and send nothing.

Permissions and what they're used for

  • Notifications / alarms – to ring your alarms and show reminders.
  • Microphone and speech recognition – only while you describe your alarms by voice.
  • Motion & fitness / activity recognition – for sleep estimates and the Shake and Step Counter wake methods. Processed on your device.
  • Camera – only for the Photo Match wake method. Photos stay on your device.
  • Calendar (Premium, optional) – to create alarms before your events. Read on your device, never uploaded.
  • Approximate location (optional) – for the alarm-time weather forecast.

Advertising and consent

The free version shows banner and full-screen ads. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) you are asked for consent before personalised ads, and on iPhone you choose whether to allow tracking through Apple's prompt; you can change these choices in the app's Settings and in your device settings. Premium removes ads.

Premium and subscriptions

Premium is available as a yearly or monthly subscription (the yearly plan can include a free trial) or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel, at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we only receive confirmation of what you bought and its status. On the App Store, the yearly plan can be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

Children

Snoozle is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Uninstalling Snoozle deletes everything stored on your device.
  • Switching off AI features stops all AI requests immediately.
  • Advertising and analytics data are kept by the providers above under their own policies. To ask us to delete data linked to your anonymous IDs, email under.rated.ruan@gmail.com with your device type and approximate install date. We respond within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, POPIA in South Africa, or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Email us to use these rights.

Security

Data sent by the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The AI service key is stored only on our server.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
