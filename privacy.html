Snoozle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026 · Contact: under.rated.ruan@gmail.com

Snoozle ("the app") is an alarm clock app for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Where something applies to only one platform, it says so.

Information that stays on your device

Your alarms, settings, wake-up streaks and stats, Morning Reports, sleep estimates, the reference photo for Photo Match, and calendar event titles and times used by Calendar Sync are stored only on your device. We don't run servers and there are no accounts, so we can't see or access this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions and what they're used for

  • Notifications and alarms: to ring your alarms and show reminders.
  • Approximate location (optional): to show a weather forecast for your alarm time. Your approximate coordinates are sent to the weather service Open-Meteo to get the forecast. We don't store them.
  • Calendar (optional): to create alarms before your calendar events. Read-only, and never uploaded.
  • Camera (optional): only for the Photo Match wake-up challenge. Photos stay on your device.
  • Motion and physical activity (optional): for the Shake and Step Counter challenges and for sleep tracking. Processed on your device.
  • Microphone (Android only, optional): while sleep tracking is on, the app takes short sound-level samples to notice loud noises such as snoring. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Only a "was it loud" result is kept on your device.

Sleep estimates

Sleep figures in Snoozle are rough estimates based on your phone's movement (and, on Android, sound levels if you allow the microphone). They are not medical measurements and aren't intended to diagnose or treat any condition.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, approximate location and information about how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. Where required by law, you'll be asked for consent first, and you can change your choice later from "Privacy & ad choices" in the app's settings. On iOS you also choose whether to allow tracking through Apple's prompt, and can change it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google or privacy settings. Snoozle Premium removes ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Usage analytics (Android only)

The Android app uses GameAnalytics to understand how the app is used, for example installs, sessions, app version, device type and operating system, and in-app events such as ads shown. This helps us improve the app. GameAnalytics processes this data under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Snoozle Premium is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play (Android) or Apple (iOS). We don't receive your payment details.

Children

Snoozle is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

Every permission above is optional apart from notifications and alarms, which the app needs to ring. You can turn permissions off at any time in your device settings; the related feature will simply stop working. To delete the data stored on your device, uninstall the app. For questions about data held by Google or GameAnalytics, see their policies linked above, or contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do.
