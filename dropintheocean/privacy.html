Drop in the Ocean – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Drop in the Ocean ("the game") is made by RVV Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. Contact: under.rated.ruan@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your island, factories, coins, gems, pearls, fish collection, progress and settings are saved on your device and never sent to us.
  • The game measures how it is played (for example which water bodies are reached, how long sessions are and which ad rewards are used) with GameAnalytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, using anonymous identifiers. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland this only starts after the consent step.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob, using an advertising ID and anonymous identifiers. Most ads are optional rewarded videos you choose to watch.
  • The game currently has no in-app purchases. If purchases are added, they will be handled by Google Play or the App Store and this policy will be updated.
  • There is no chat, no messaging and no public profile.
  • You can change your ad choices any time under Settings ▸ Privacy options (where available) and in your device settings.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use the data to identify you personally, and we do not combine it with other data about you.

Advertising and consent

Most ads are optional rewarded videos: you choose to watch one, for example to double what your island earned while you were away or to double your income for a few hours. Occasionally a short full-screen ad appears at a natural break, such as when a new body of water opens, never in the middle of play and never in your first day of playing. Ads are limited to content suitable for ages 13 and up.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, personalised ads and analytics stay off unless you agree; the consent form appears before any ads load, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy options. Everywhere else, personalised ads and analytics run by default; you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads; iOS: Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking), and you can ask us to delete data linked to your anonymous IDs. On iPhone, the game asks whether apps may track you (Apple's App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown.

Children

Drop in the Ocean is intended for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your device, including your progress.
  • Analytics and advertising data is kept by the providers above under their own retention policies. To ask for deletion of data linked to your anonymous IDs, email under.rated.ruan@gmail.com and tell us your device type and approximate install date. We respond within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or certain US states), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising or the "sale or sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To use any of these rights, email us; you can also change your ad choices in Settings ▸ Privacy options and your device settings, and you can complain to your local data-protection authority.

Security

Data sent by the game travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to the providers above.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example if we add purchases, another ad network or analytics tool), we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the game.
