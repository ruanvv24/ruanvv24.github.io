Fuse – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Fuse ("the game") is made by RVV Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. Contact: under.rated.ruan@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your real name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • In Fuse City you pick a username (a nickname, not your real name). Friends you add can see your username, your town and your town level.
  • Your progress is saved on your device.
  • The game shows ads (through Unity LevelPlay: ironSource Ads and Unity Ads) and measures how the game is played (GameAnalytics), using an advertising ID and anonymous identifiers.
  • The online features (Daily Fuse leaderboard, friends, visiting towns) use Unity Gaming Services with an anonymous player ID and your username plus a short tag, such as "Mayor_Ruan#4821".
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • You can turn analytics off in Settings, and (in the EU/UK) change your ad consent under Settings ▸ Privacy options.

Advertising and consent

Ads are provided through Unity LevelPlay, which works with ad networks including ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent using Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown, and passes your choice to these networks; you can change your choice at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy options. Elsewhere you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads; iOS: Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking). Buying Remove Ads turns off ad breaks and banners; rewarded ads remain optional.

Ad partners' policy (Unity LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads are Unity services): https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Analytics

GameAnalytics receives anonymous gameplay events so we can balance levels and fix problems. Switch it off any time in Settings ▸ Analytics.

GameAnalytics' policy: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy

On iPhone and iPad the game also uses Google Analytics for Firebase only to see whether our own Google ads lead to installs. It starts with all consent signals off, follows your consent choice, and stops when you switch off Settings ▸ Analytics. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Achievements (Android)

On Android the game signs in to Google Play Games automatically, the way most Play games do, and unlocks Fuse achievements there as you reach them. Your Play Games profile and achievements are managed by Google under its policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can turn off automatic sign-in or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app.

Leaderboard

When you open or join the Daily Fuse leaderboard, the game signs in anonymously with Unity Gaming Services and shows your username (or a generated name).

Usernames, friends and visiting towns

  • Your username must be 3–14 letters, numbers or underscores, and the game blocks offensive words. Unity adds a short tag (for example #4821); the whole thing is your friend code.
  • Only people who have your friend code can send you a friend request, and you choose whether to accept it. There is no public search, chat or messaging.
  • Friends you accept can see your username, town level and a copy of your town, buy from your roadside stall, and help your town once a day.
  • To report a username or a player, email under.rated.ruan@gmail.com with their friend code. We review reports and can remove names or accounts that break these rules.

Unity's policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Children

Fuse is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your device. Your online record (username, friends, town copy) stays with Unity Gaming Services until you ask us to delete it.
  • Analytics and leaderboard data are kept by the providers above under their retention policies. To ask for deletion of data linked to your anonymous IDs, email under.rated.ruan@gmail.com (include your friend code or the name shown in the game). We respond within 30 days.

Security

Data sent by the game travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to the providers above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the game.
