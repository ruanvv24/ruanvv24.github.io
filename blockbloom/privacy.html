Block Bloom – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 (ads now come from Google AdMob only; consent through Google's consent form)

Block Bloom ("the game") is made by RVV Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. Contact: under.rated.ruan@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress (coins, levels, boosters, settings) is saved on your device.
  • The game shows ads (Google AdMob from version 1.0.3; earlier versions used Unity LevelPlay with Unity Ads and ironSource Ads) and measures how the game is played (GameAnalytics), using an advertising ID and anonymous identifiers.
  • On Android, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count installs and opens and to note when a player clears their early levels, so we can see which of our Google Ads bring real players.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • There is no chat, no messaging and no public profile.
  • You can turn analytics off in Settings, and change your ad choice any time under Settings ▸ Privacy choices (EEA, UK, Switzerland) or Settings ▸ Personal ads (elsewhere).

Advertising and consent

Ads are delivered by Google AdMob (versions before 1.0.3 used Unity LevelPlay, which picked ads from Unity Ads and ironSource Ads). They appear as optional rewarded videos (you choose to watch one for a reward), as short full-screen ads between some game rounds, and as a banner at the bottom of the play screen.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland (and wherever the game cannot tell your region), Google's consent form asks on first launch, before any ad is requested, whether your data may be used to personalise ads and measure the game (versions before 1.0.3 asked the same question on the game's own screen). If you say no you still see ads, but not personalised ones. Everywhere else personalised ads are on by default. You can change your choice, or opt out of personalised ads (including the sale or sharing of data for advertising under US state laws), at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy choices (EEA, UK, Switzerland) or Settings ▸ Personal ads (elsewhere). On iPhone, the game asks whether apps may track you (Apple's App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown. Elsewhere you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads; iOS: Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking).

Buying Remove Ads turns off the full-screen ads and the banner. Rewarded videos stay optional.

Unity's policy (for versions before 1.0.3): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Analytics

GameAnalytics receives gameplay events linked to an anonymous ID so we can balance levels and fix problems. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it only starts after you agree in the first-launch consent form. You can switch it off at any time in Settings ▸ Analytics.

GameAnalytics' policy: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy

Ad measurement (Google Analytics for Firebase, Android)

On Android, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase only to measure which of our Google Ads campaigns lead to players who actually play: the automatic install ("first open") and session events, the end of each run (game mode and score) and the first time each Adventure level is cleared. Firebase receives a random app-instance identifier, your advertising ID when allowed, device and app information and your approximate country. It starts switched off with every consent signal set to "denied", and only starts once your privacy choice is known: outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland straight away, inside only after you agree in the first-launch consent form. Switching off Settings ▸ Analytics also keeps it off. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

In-app purchases (such as Remove Ads, the Starter Pack and coin packs) are processed by Google Play or the App Store. We receive confirmation that a purchase happened, the product and the price, but never your card or payment details. Restore in the shop gives your purchases back on a new device.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example when the daily challenge resets). They are not sent from a server. You can switch them off in the game's Settings or in your device settings.

Children

Block Bloom is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your device.
  • Analytics and advertising data are kept by the providers above under their own retention policies. To ask for deletion of data linked to your anonymous IDs, email under.rated.ruan@gmail.com and tell us your device type and approximate install date. We respond within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or certain US states), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising or the "sale or sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To use any of these rights, email us; you can also change your ad choice in Settings ▸ Privacy choices (EEA, UK, Switzerland) or Settings ▸ Personal ads (elsewhere), and you can complain to your local data-protection authority.

Security

Data sent by the game travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to the providers above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the game.
