Slideways: Street Drift – Support

Questions, a bug, or a purchase that didn't arrive? Email under.rated.ruan@gmail.com and we'll reply as soon as we can. Please include your phone model and the version shown in the App Store or Google Play.

Purchases

No Ads and the Starter Pack are one-time purchases. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open SHOP on the main menu and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from (Apple or Google Play).

Controls

Steer with the arc at the bottom left, or switch Settings ▸ STEERING to TAP SIDES and hold the left or right half of the screen. Auto-throttle is on by default; turn it off in Settings to get a gas pedal. Tap the handbrake to start a slide.

Online ghosts and driver names

Career and daily events show the ghost of another player's best run. Set your driver name in Settings. If a name is offensive, tap REPORT on the results screen. To stop sharing your runs, switch GHOST: OFF in the Career screen.
