Slideways: Street Drift – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Slideways: Street Drift ("the game") is made by RVV Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. Contact: under.rated.ruan@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress (cash, best scores, stars, cars, settings) is saved on your device.
  • The game shows ads (Google AdMob) and measures how the game is played (GameAnalytics), using an advertising ID and anonymous identifiers.
  • On Android, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count installs and opens and to note when a player finishes runs, so we can see which of our Google Ads bring real players.
  • On Android you can sign in to Google Play Games (optional) for achievements and leaderboards. Your Play Games profile name and scores are shown on the leaderboards; we never see your email.
  • Any purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • There is no chat, no messaging and no public profile.
  • You can turn analytics off in Settings and change your ad choice under Settings ▸ Privacy options (EEA, UK, Switzerland).

Advertising and consent

Ads are delivered by Google AdMob. They appear as optional rewarded videos (you choose to watch one, for example to double the cash from a run) and occasionally as a short full-screen ad on the results screen between runs. Ads never appear while you are driving.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland (and wherever the game cannot tell your region), Google's consent form asks on first launch, before any ad is requested, whether your data may be used to personalise ads and measure the game. If you say no you still see ads, but not personalised ones. You can change your choice at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy options. On iPhone, the game asks whether apps may track you (Apple's App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads; iOS: Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking).

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Analytics

GameAnalytics receives gameplay events linked to an anonymous ID so we can balance the game and fix problems. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it only starts after you agree in the first-launch consent form. You can switch it off at any time in Settings ▸ Analytics.

GameAnalytics' policy: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy

Ad measurement (Google Analytics for Firebase, Android)

On Android, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase only to measure which of our Google Ads campaigns lead to players who actually play: the automatic install ("first open") and session events and the end of each run (challenge and score). Firebase receives a random app-instance identifier, your advertising ID when allowed, device and app information and your approximate country. It starts switched off with every consent signal set to "denied" and only starts once your privacy choice is known: outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland straight away, inside only after you agree in the first-launch consent form. Switching off Settings ▸ Analytics also keeps it off. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from apps.

Google Play Games (Android, optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game sends Google your unlocked achievements, your daily challenge scores and your biggest banked combo. Google shows them in its achievements and leaderboard screens, where other players can see your Play Games profile name, avatar and scores. We only see your Play Games player ID and scores, never your email address. You can play without signing in, and manage your Play Games profile and its visibility in the Google Play Games app. See Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, they are processed by Google Play or the App Store. We receive confirmation that a purchase happened, the product and the price, but never your card or payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your device. To ask for data held by GameAnalytics or Google to be deleted, email us at under.rated.ruan@gmail.com and we will help.

Changes

We will update this page if the game starts using information differently, and change the date at the top.
