Crossroads – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Crossroads: Choose Your Story ("the game") is made by RVV Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. Contact: under.rated.ruan@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The name you give your character, your look, outfits, gems, energy, story progress, save slots and settings are saved on your device and never sent to us.
  • The game measures how it is used (which stories and chapters are played, where players stop) with GameAnalytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, using anonymous identifiers. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland this only starts after you agree; elsewhere it is on by default and you can limit it in your device settings.
  • The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (with the ad networks ironSource Ads and Unity Ads), using an advertising ID and anonymous identifiers.
  • After your first chapter the game can ask to send you reminder notifications. They are scheduled on your device, nothing is sent from a server, and you can say no or switch them off at any time.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • There is no chat, no messaging and no public profile.
  • You can change your ad choices any time under Settings ▸ Privacy options (where available) and in your device settings.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use the data to identify you personally, and we do not combine it with other data about you.

Advertising and consent

Ads appear as optional rewarded videos (you choose to watch one, for example to refill energy) and occasionally as a short full-screen ad at the end of a chapter, never in the middle of a scene. Ads are limited to content suitable for ages 13 and up. VIP members and owners of a paid book do not see ads in that book.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, personalised ads and analytics stay off unless you agree (the game also treats a device that looks European, for example by SIM country, language or time zone, as inside these regions). When the consent form is shown you can change your choice at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy options. Everywhere else, personalised ads and analytics run by default; you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings, and you can ask us to delete data linked to your anonymous IDs. On iPhone, the game asks whether apps may track you (Apple's App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads; iOS: Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking).

Reminder notifications

After you finish your first chapter the game asks whether it may send notifications (Android 13 and later, and iPhone, show a system question; you can answer no). If you agree, the game schedules a few reminders on your phone, for example a day, three days and a week after you leave, and on your first days also a short while after you leave. They are only shown between 10:00 and 20:00 local time and say which chapter is waiting. They are created on your device: no push server and no personal data are involved. We only count, anonymously, whether you allowed notifications and whether you opened the game from one. Switch them off any time in your phone's notification settings for Crossroads.

Purchases

In-app purchases (gems, the Starter Pack, premium books and the VIP subscription) are processed by Google Play or the App Store. We receive confirmation that a purchase happened and the product, but never your card or payment details. Restore purchases in Settings gives your purchases back on a new device. The VIP subscription renews automatically until you cancel it in your Google Play or Apple ID account settings.

Sharing a scene

When you tap Share, the game creates a picture of the current scene and opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Children

Crossroads is intended for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Settings ▸ Delete all saves and restart removes your progress from the device; uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your device.
  • Advertising data is kept by the providers above under their own retention policies. To ask for deletion of data linked to your anonymous IDs, email under.rated.ruan@gmail.com and tell us your device type and approximate install date. We respond within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or certain US states), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising or the "sale or sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To use any of these rights, email us; you can also change your ad choices in Settings ▸ Privacy options and your device settings, and you can complain to your local data-protection authority.

Security

Data sent by the game travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to the providers above.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example if we add another ad network or analytics tool), we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the game.
